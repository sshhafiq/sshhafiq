About this tracker

This tracker measures the number of applications submitted through McDonald's U.S. job pages. Values are aggregated per hour, day, or month depending on the selected range.

The moving average is a trailing simple average whose window scales with the range. Period change compares the last interval to the first; volatility is the coefficient of variation across intervals.
